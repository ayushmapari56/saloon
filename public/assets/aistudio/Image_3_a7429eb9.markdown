## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a sanctuary of bespoke luxury, bespoke hair artistry, and holistic aesthetic wellness. Evoking the tactile quietude of high-fashion salon salons and Parisian beauty publications, the aesthetic combines deliberate editorial restraint with tactile warmth. 

The visual voice targets discerning luxury clientele seeking transformative salon treatments and couture cosmetic formulations. Key emotional resonances are calm exclusivity, artistic authority, and sensory indulgence. 

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Luxury**:
- Expansive, breathing white space mimicking heavy rag paper and editorial print layouts.
- Hairline structural framing and delicate rules separating services, pricing, and visual portfolios.
- Balanced contrasts between deep espresso-charcoal text and incandescent alabaster-sand surfaces.
- Soft champagne-gold touches used purely for precise focus, status indicators, and booking accents.

## Colors
The palette evokes raw linen, spun silk, and polished volcanic marble, avoiding cold clinical whites in favor of radiant, warm alabaster undertones:

- **Primary (`#1A1615` - Deep Espresso Noir):** Serves as the high-contrast foundational ink for editorial headlines, primary structural borders, and resolute call-to-actions.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` - Champagne Pale Gold):** Applied selectively to interactive highlights, delicate decorative rules, active toggle indicators, and prestigious salon tier badges.
- **Tertiary (`#7D6B5D` - Muted Umber Taupe):** Provides nuanced contrast for sub-headings, service durations, editorial captions, and secondary metadata.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Warm Alabaster Canvas):** Forms the foundational background atmosphere, reflecting natural architectural light and tactile vellum paper.

Complementary interior neutrals include `#EFECE6` (Sand Canvas for subtle nested containers) and `#E5DFC5` (Fine Hairline Border tone).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm depends on an intentional high-fashion tension between the grand, sculpted serifs of `Playfair Display` and the contemporary, pristine legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Use `Playfair Display` with standard or italicized weights to convey bespoke craftsmanship. Large headlines should maintain relaxed leading and slight negative tracking for dramatic presence.
- **Body & Editorial Copy:** Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` with lighter weights (`300` and `400`) and increased line heights to ensure effortlessly legible passages on sand and alabaster backdrops.
- **Labels, Badges, & Metatags:** Use uppercase styling with wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) on `label-sm` and `label-md` to emulate haute couture product labels and high-end atelier tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetrical, editorial column format inspired by premium print design.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `4rem` outer canvas padding and `2rem` gutters. Sections utilize generous vertical rhythmic cadences (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to let high-resolution photography and typography breathe without dense grouping.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Rebalances to an 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Staggered imagery reflows into structured split-screens.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Adapts to a 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Section vertical spacing reduces to `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to preserve editorial proportions while maintaining swift navigation for mobile booking flows.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system is communicated through warm tonal layering and soft ambient luminosity, deliberately avoiding dark drop shadows or clinical layered elevation:

- **Surface Tonal Layers:** Depth relies on layered chromatic values: `Canvas (#F9F6F0)` holds foundational content, `Surface Layer (#F3EFE8)` holds card interiors and drawers, and `Active Raised (#FFFFFF)` serves modals and high-focus booking overlays.
- **Micro Hairline Borders:** Interfaces achieve definition through ultra-fine 1px borders rendered in `#E5DFC5` or 12% opacity Deep Espresso (`rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.12)`).
- **Ambient Champagne Halos:** For floating navigation, booking modals, and active service selections, apply an expansive, ultra-diffused shadow tinted with sand and warm umber tones: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(125, 107, 93, 0.08), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.03)`.

## Shapes
The shape hierarchy pairs architectural, rectilinear frames with purposeful curved accents:

- **Cards, Photography, and Section Modules:** Standardize on Level 1 softness (`0.25rem` / `4px`) or razor-sharp crisp edges (`0px`), evoking traditional picture mounts, gallery frames, and structural editorial blocks.
- **Interactive Badges & Meta Pills:** Use full pill rounding (`9999px`) exclusively for badges, category filters, and treatment indicators to contrast against the sharp structural grids.
- **Form Controls & Action Buttons:** Maintain crisp, architectural profiles (`2px` to `4px` maximum radius) to preserve an understated salon sophistication.

## Components

### Buttons & Call to Actions
- **Primary Appointment CTA:** Deep Espresso Noir (`#1A1615`) background, Warm Alabaster (`#F9F6F0`) typography in `label-lg`, minimal padding `1rem 2.25rem`, subtle `2px` corner rounding. Hover state smoothly transitions background to `#332D2B` with a subtle `+1px` letter spacing shift.
- **Secondary / Editorial Outline:** Transparent background, surrounded by a 1px `#1A1615` border. Transitions on hover to `#1A1615` fill with inverse text.
- **Tertiary Champagne Link:** Understated text link in `label-md` with a persistent 1px hairline underline in `#C5A880`, offset by 6px.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- Rendered using full pill geometry (`roundedness: 9999px`) with padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`.
- Applied for specialist designations ("Master Stylist", "Couture Hair Spa") using `#EFECE6` background, `#7D6B5D` text, and uppercase `label-sm` tracking.

### Service Menu & Pricing Rows
- Clean, open-row layout separated by 1px `#E5DFC5` hairlines.
- Left column hosts the service title in `headline-sm` with duration underneath in `body-sm` (`#7D6B5D`).
- Right column presents the price in `price-editorial` paired with an elegant, circular "+" booking trigger or understated "Reserve" text.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Border-bottom editorial styling: flat transparent background framed by a single bottom rule (`1px solid #C5A880`).
- Floating placeholder labels transitioning to `label-sm` upon focus.
- Date and time slot selectors styled as low-contrast architectural tiles that transition to Deep Espresso fill when selected.

### Product & Portfolio Cards
- Generous vertical aspect ratios (4:5 or 3:4) with hairline edge borders.
- Image overlay treatments use soft alabaster gradients rather than dark backdrops.
- Product listings highlight delicate cosmetic bottles and luxury packaging with generous surrounding white space and micro price tags.
